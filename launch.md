# Tasks: A Simple To-Do List That Stays Out of Your Way

Meet Tasks, a small to-do app that lives in a single HTML file. There is nothing to install and no account to create. Open it, type what needs doing, and press Enter.

We built Tasks for people who want a short list they can trust. It remembers your tasks between visits by saving them in your browser, so your list is still there when you come back. The header shows how many tasks are active and how many are done, and the footer counts what's left.

Here's what you can do:

- **Add, check off, and delete tasks** with one click, or entirely from the keyboard.
- **Edit a task in place** by double-clicking it (or tapping the pencil on touch screens). Press Enter to save or Escape to cancel.
- **Filter your list** by All, Active, or Done. Tasks remembers which view you picked.
- **Clear completed tasks** in one go when you want a fresh start.
- **Light and dark themes** that follow your system setting, with reduced motion respected too.

Open index.html in your browser and start checking things off.
